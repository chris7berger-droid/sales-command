- **B-archive-grant-anon** — **`archive` schema `GRANT ALL … TO anon` with ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES** (carry-over)
  - `sql/history_locker_phase1.sql:118-122`
  - Evidence: `GRANT USAGE ON SCHEMA archive TO anon, authenticated; GRANT ALL ON ALL TABLES IN SCHEMA archive TO anon, authenticated; GRANT ALL ON ALL SEQUENCES IN SCHEMA archive TO anon, authenticated; ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES IN SCHEMA archive GRANT ALL ON TABLES TO anon, authenticated;`

  - Fix: `GRANT ... TO authenticated` only (drop anon); scope every RLS policy with `TO authenticated`; `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES ... TO authenticated` only.

- **C-qb-record-payment-idempotency** — **`qb-record-payment` never checks `qb_payment_id` before POSTing to QB `/payment` — reprocessed Stripe events book duplicate QB Payments** (NEW / elevated from prior LOW C-stripe-webhook-reclaim)
  - `supabase/functions/qb-record-payment/index.ts:141-175`
  - Evidence: handler gates on `!invoice.qb_invoice_id` at :141 but has NO check on `invoice.qb_payment_id`. QB POST at :170; `qb_payment_id` only persisted AFTER the POST at :175.
  - `stripe-webhook` reprocess branch (:101, "claimed"→re-process) invokes `qb-record-payment` on every retry. - **C-qb-record-payment-net** — **`qb-record-payment` `netAmount` still ignores `retention_amount` — books gross-minus-discount instead of net collected** (carry-over — elevated to HIGH; money integrity)
  - `supabase/functions/qb-record-payment/index.ts:153`
  - Evidence: `const netAmount = (invoice.amount || 0) - (invoice.discount || 0);`
  - `send-invoice/index.ts:161` computes the Stripe-charged amount as `amount - discount - retentionAmount`. When Stripe fires the webhook and `qb-record-payment` books the payment, it reads directly from `invoices` and excludes retention — QB Payment posts for `amount - discount` which INCLUDES retention that was never collected. On any retention invoice this over-books QB by the retention amount, leaves A/R short by that amount, and the eventual release invoice double-counts. Third audit cycle. Elevated to HIGH: money-reconciliation defect on a shipped path.
  - Spec: OWASP-ASVS V13.2.4, CWE-682, money integrity.
  - Fix: `const netAmount = (invoice.amount || 0) - (invoice.discount || 0) - (Number(invoice.retention_amount) || 0);` (matches send-invoice) — or use `session.amount_total / 100` passed via the webhook.
