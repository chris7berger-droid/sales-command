## Top 3 Urgent

1. **`_shared/repNotify.ts:153` — HTML injection via customer-controlled `signerName` and admin-controlled `reason` (NEW HIGH).** The shared rep-notification helper interpolates `${how.signerName || "—"}` and `${how.reason || "—"}` directly into the HTML email sent to sales reps on every proposal approval — no `esc()` helper (`follow-up-reminders/index.ts:48` defines one and uses it consistently). A customer at the public signing page can set `signer_name` to `<a href="https://phish.example">click to confirm</a>` and the rep receives what looks like a legitimate Sales Command email carrying the attacker's payload. Fix: define an `esc()` helper and wrap every `${...}` interpolation (customer, jobLabel, signerName, approvedBy, reason, rep.name).
2. **`src/pages/Team.jsx:32` — any sales-app user creates Admin `team_members` rows (NEW HIGH — pairs with B11 legacy-seed RLS).** `handleSave` inserts/updates `team_members` including `role` and `apps` with no client role gate; the `/sales/team` route is only gated by `GroupGuard app="sales"`. Combined with the still-permissive `team_members_update` RLS at `sql/rls_identity_tables.sql:64` (prod claim of fix in `command-suite-db` migration `20260722120000`; seed still ships anti-pattern), any authenticated sales-app user can create Admin rows or elevate themselves. Direct vertical privilege escalation. Fix: gate `handleSave` on `teamMember.role === "Admin"`, hide the Team route from non-Admin nav, and verify the RLS lockdown is applied both in prod and in this repo's seed.
3. **`supabase/functions/qb-record-payment/index.ts:167` — `netAmount` does not subtract retention (carry-over — upgraded MEDIUM→HIGH).** `send-invoice/index.ts:161` computes the customer-facing Stripe amount as `amount - discount - retentionAmount`; `qb-record-payment` records `amount - discount`. Every retention invoice paid through Stripe now posts a QB Payment larger than what was actually collected — phantom balance and misstated A/R on every retention deal since the feature shipped. Money-math correctness defect in active use. Fix: `const netAmount = (invoice.amount || 0) - (invoice.discount || 0) - (Number(invoice.retention_amount) || 0);` and ensure `retention_amount` is included in the `.select('*')` at `:108`.
