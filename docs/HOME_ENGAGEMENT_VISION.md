## The one goal, in one line

**Drive estimators to engage in the work the business needs done — not their own default habits.**
Everything on the screen serves that.

## Governing principles (locked in the ideate)

1. **Designed around the business, not roles.** ONE screen, same for everyone. No manager view, no
   Team view, no role dashboards. The personal parts personalize to whoever's logged in; the crew
   runway and shared goal are simply on the screen, so everyone sees them. Fits the brand —
   *"helping craftsmen become businessmen."*
2. **Urgency shows through movement + color — never by removing encouragement.** When things get
   tense, elements slide up and change color; the person always keeps their anchor. Even a crisis
   reads as "here's where you shine," not "the sky is falling."
3. **Never promise what the tool can't deliver.** The screen talks *to* them; it does not pretend to
   converse. A broken expectation costs the trust and the user. (Killed the back-and-forth check-in
   for this reason.)
4. **Reframe every weak spot as opportunity.** Same data, opposite feeling. Not "$30K behind" →
   "$30K to goal — one good job." Not "you dropped this" → "$40K last year, go get it."
5. **Obvious and easy, video-game bold.** See north-star above.

## The assembled screen — top to bottom (it *breathes*: in to *you*, out to the *business*, back in to *your moves*)

1. **YOU (hero) — always #1, never displaced.** A win that reads their month. Good month → results +
   records ("$312K this year, best month ever"). Bad month → **effort** ("18 calls, 5 bids out —
   you're doing the work, it's coming"). Always true, always positive, never empty. This is the
   confidence hit *before* anything is asked of them.
2. **YOUR MONEY (#2 normally).** One **month progress bar** filled by bookings (managed monthly, so a
   slow week isn't a visible failure) + a **moving pace marker** ("where you should be by now") whose
   color warms and gap grows as the weeks run out. Beside it a **clickable donut** that switches
   between: work type · big-vs-small · booked-vs-remaining. Gap always spoken as opportunity + the
   next move ("$30K to goal — one good job, or 6 quiet bids you already have out").
3. **THE BUSINESS, IN THE OPEN.** The **crew runway** (everyone sees it; a manager/admin enters it —
   calm when booked out, tension-colored when thin) and the **shared goal** thermometer to $100K (one
   rope, no ranking — natural competition self-surfaces privately). *When the runway goes critical it
   slides up above the money and turns its tension color — no takeover, the hero win stays put.*
4. **YOUR BOOK (scoreboard) — the doorway.** A calm, **dollars-forward** pipeline strip (Wants Bid →
   Has Bid → Sold) with dollars + a count per stage, each **tappable to drill straight into that
   pile.** Read where your money's parked, tap to go work it.
5. **WHAT YOU OWE.** Follow-ups = **the dates they set themselves + bids due** (no auto call-backs).
   Finite, checkable, and it **celebrates when clear** ("all caught up — go hunt"). Clearing it is
   visible proof of effort.
6. **WHERE TO HUNT.** The **served opportunity** (below) + the existing dormant / gone-quiet call
   lists, each spiced with the dollar number that makes a call feel like chasing money.

## The opportunity finder ("coach, not scoreboard")

Turns the screen from *here's your number* into *here's your move.* It **serves one** automatically
(auto-ranked strongest — reaches the *unmotivated* rep who'd never go looking), with a **Refresh** that
**names its criteria** each time and serves the next angle. **Non-destructive** — step back / pin, so
they can return to the best of the ones they saw. Every nudge **shows its work** ("because you sold
$X to them last year and nothing this year"). Tap → drops them straight into that call, pre-spiced.

The refresh cycles a pre-built set of **10 angles** (ship 3–4 first, grow later):

1. **Biggest Bid Hanging** — largest quiet bid (sent, 30+ days, not won).
2. **Almost Yes** — a bid they *opened* but never signed (uses proposal view tracking we have).
3. **Quick Win** — smallest/oldest quiet bid; an easy roll-start on a slow day.
4. **The One That Got Cold** — dormant past customer, highest past dollars. *(QB deepens the $)*
5. **You're Their Guy** — the customer they've personally done the most jobs with, gone quiet.
6. **Down From Last Year** — biggest year-over-year drop. *(needs QB)*
7. **Chase a Whale** — a past big-project customer with nothing in the pipe now (drives goal #2).
8. **New Blood** — a fresh inquiry sitting untouched.
9. **Play to Your Strength** — their most-sold work type → a quiet customer of that type.
10. **Neighborhood Run** — a cluster in one city/zip, so they batch a day of calls.

Held in reserve: Follow-Up Due Today, Streak Saver.

## Scope — what closes this out (RATIFIED by Chris 2026-08-12)

**Design-only decision. The build is one contained session that reskins Home into the shape above +
adds read-only selectors + two charts. NO migrations, NO backend, NO new architecture.** The engine
(snapshot loader, dormant/gone-quiet lists, suppression memory, follow-up + bid-due logic) is reused
as-is. The database is **fully reused — zero new migrations** — because everything reads tables that
already exist and the snapshot already fetches. Runway uses the single `schedule_runway_weeks` number
already pushed.

| Ships now (reskin + cheap read-only selectors, zero DB) | Deferred (needs new data/plumbing) |
|---|---|
| Hero win · month bar + pace marker + work-type donut | QuickBooks "$ last year vs this year" spice + QB-dependent angles (#4 rich, #6) |
| Runway bar (the single number already stored) | The full 10 refresh angles — ship 3–4, grow later |
| Shared goal thermometer · scoreboard strip w/ drill-in | Per-week crew counts + who's-available + the 72h/30% critical math |
| Follow-ups + bids-due (already built) · dormant/quiet lists + served opportunity (3–4 angles) | |

The **rich runway** (per-week crew, who's available, real 72h/30% countdown — thresholds already
belong in Settings) is **not cut — Schedule Command auto-feeds it later.** Building elaborate manual
crew entry now would be throwaway work. One number is the right call.

## Carry-outs (separate from this build)

- **Cash flow forecast → AR Command**, not this screen. Right driver, wrong home.
- **"Those numbers can't be right"** on the current Sales Dashboard (cash flow / analytics) — a bug
  to verify + log to BACKLOG, separate from this design.
- **SalesDash** is legacy (only cash flow + analytics were ever used). Its useful pieces are
  candidates to migrate/retire later; don't prop it up.

## Two audiences, one screen

- **Manager** — the only difference is a *permission*: they can enter the crew runway. Same screen,
  same experience otherwise.
- **Estimator** — everything personalizes to them.
