════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DECISIONS (Chris-ratified this session)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. Proposals top bar period basis = sent/sold date, not created date.
2. Multi-GC job = ONE $-value job in every top-bar number (you can only win one
   GC), but ALL GC copies stay visible in the list.
3. ONE sold-month rule across Proposals, Call Log, Home = the SOLD date.
   Invoices intentionally NOT included — its top bar tracks billing events
   (drafted/invoiced/collected), a different question dated by invoice dates.
4. Undateable archive sales (no recorded sold date) are dropped from sold-month
   on all three screens, not credited to their import date.
5. Rep filter drives the scoreboards, and the boards must name the rep.
