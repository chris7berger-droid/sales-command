SESSION SUMMARY
---------------
Chris asked why the PDF coming out of the WTC wizard was rounding, said he didn't want it to, and
that if rounding was deliberate it should round UP.

It was rounding for two unrelated reasons, and chasing the second one turned up the root cause of
the 2026-06-26 exact-penny project.

`ProposalPDFModal.jsx` computed the customer-facing proposal total by hand —
`totals.labor + totals.materials + totals.travel - totals.discount` — instead of calling
`calcWtcPrice`. `git log -L170,170` shows that line has never been modified since the file was
created in b969309 (the v52 component extraction), predating the exact-penny plan. Because it never
called into `calc.js`, the plan's ~15 call-site rewires walked past it.

Consequence: the customer-facing PDF printed the raw PRE-ceil sum while the invoice billed the
CEIL'd figure. That is precisely the complaint §1 of `docs/plans/exact_penny_pricing.md` was written
to solve — "customers pay the exact contract amount ≈2–3×/week while we billed the rounded-up
figure." The plan read that as evidence the round-UP was wrong and removed it from the billed side.
The causation was inverted: customers paid the un-rounded amount BECAUSE THAT IS THE NUMBER WE
PRINTED FOR THEM. The billed side was never the broken side.

With the PDF fixed to print the number it bills, round-UP is coherent again and is Chris's stated
preference for customer-facing figures. So exact-penny pricing was converted from an open-ended era
into a CLOSED WINDOW rather than being left in place.

DECISIONS / CHOICES MADE
------------------------
- Round UP going forward, not penny-exact (Chris ratified). Chris's own framing broke the problem
  open: "if the proposal had been originally sent out at 37,155, that whole problem goes away,
  correct?" — correct, and it relocated the defect from the invoice to the PDF.
- The window CLOSES rather than the era being deleted. Proposals quoted 6/26–7/29 were quoted, sent,
  and in some cases signed at penny-exact prices; repricing them would move contract amounts on
  committed work. That ~1-month cohort prints cents permanently. Accepted cost.
- `EXACT_PRICING_END` is midnight Central 7/29, NOT midnight 7/28. Checked the DB first: two
  proposals were quoted on 7/28 and one was already status=Sent to a customer at $27,443.47
  (id 72572e85-98ae-476d-8067-ee5c3494fb69). A same-morning boundary would have repriced a proposal
  already in a customer's hands. Cost of the later boundary: anything quoted the evening of 7/28
  still prices to the penny.
- Considered and REJECTED: making `call_log.show_cents` the real money switch. It would need a
  per-proposal mode column + backfill (a job can carry proposals from both eras — mixed archive +
  live jobs), the same ~15-site SELECT-coverage sweep the 6/26 work took 3 audit rounds to get
  right, and a write in both the approval path and the `mark_proposal_signed` RPC. Half a day-plus
  and a shared-DB migration versus one constant. Deferred as F41.
- Customer-level default for exact-change REJECTED (Chris): the need is rare and not a standing
  trait of any GC; "the trend is more often to rounding." Job-level only when built.
- Fixed the public signing page unasked. Out of the literal ask ("PDF in my WTC wizard") but it is
  the identical defect on the document the customer signs — one line, same class, same session.
- Multi-GC markup override deliberately NOT threaded into the PDF price
  (`calcWtcPrice(w, undefined, exact)`). That matches ProposalDetail and Invoices exactly. Passing
  the override here would have partially fixed B53 in one surface and created a NEW
  PDF-vs-stored-total mismatch. Left alone; B53 remains open and owns that.
