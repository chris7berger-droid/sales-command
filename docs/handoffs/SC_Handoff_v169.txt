SESSION SUMMARY
---------------
Chris reported that on job 10220 (Boys & Girls Club Commercial Kitchen — Urethane Cement), the
sister proposal cloned to Peerless Construction still had KalB Construction wired into its
recipients.

The data was correct. Every read of it was wrong.

`clone_proposal_to_gcs` had done its job: the sister carries its own `proposals.customer_id`
(Peerless), its own `proposals.customer` ("Peerless Construction, Ltd"), and the right
`proposal_recipients` row (Vince Mack, vince@peerlessconst.com, signer). But `MultiGCWizard.jsx:126`
was the ONLY consumer in the entire codebase reading `proposals.customer_id`. Every other surface
resolved the customer as `call_log.customer_id` — which is permanently the parent GC, by design.

The visible symptom was cosmetic. The one underneath it was not: `send-proposal` validated the
recipient against the PARENT customer's contacts, so sending a sister proposal to any genuine
contact at its own GC would have been rejected 400 "Recipient email not associated with this
customer." Sister sends were hard-blocked, not merely mis-defaulted. Nobody had hit it because
nobody had sent one.

Four sites fixed to `proposals.customer_id ?? call_log.customer_id`. Merged and deployed at Chris's
explicit instruction. Chris then asked for the Multi-GC wizard's RFP # to stop being required;
shipped as a separate commit.

  Peerless receives, three lines from code already being changed.

  (`invoiceRow.proposals.call_log.customer_id`) — see NOT TOUCHED.
