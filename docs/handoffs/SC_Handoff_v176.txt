SESSION SUMMARY
---------------
Chris: "In pulling a History Locker job forward in the wizard, I added the billing contact,
but it did not save to the system. When I went to create the invoice, the billing contact was
not there, and when I go to customers and I open up the customer, the contact is also not
there. This is for job 6653."

One reported bug, three found. All three shared a shape: something the UI accepted, and the
save path silently had no branch for.

1. THE IMPORT WIZARD SAVED THE CONTACT & BILLING STEP ONLY FOR NEW CUSTOMERS.
   `ImportToLiveWizard.handleImport()` wrote contact/billing fields inside
   `if (form.customerMode === "new")` and had no existing-customer counterpart. Import onto an
   existing customer and the entire step — billing contact, billing terms, job contact
   phone/email — was collected, validated as REQUIRED, shown on the Review step, and then
   dropped on the floor. Nothing errored. Job 6653 (Incline Property Management) landed with
   zero `customer_contacts` rows and a null `billing_name`, which is why invoice creation had
   no billing contact to resolve. `NewInquiryWizard` got exactly this fix in Loop #28
   (2026-05-28) and it was never mirrored here.

2. TYPING A BILLING CONTACT NULLED THE JOB'S BILLING ADDRESS. Same step, different mechanism.
   `ContactBillingPicker`'s `onBillingChange` patch carries `billingSame: false` — in the picker
   that means "billing contact differs from job contact." This wizard spread the patch straight
   into form state, where `billingSame` means something else entirely: the billing-ADDRESS
   toggle read at the `call_log` insert. So the first keystroke in the Billing Contact Name
   field flipped `billing_address_same` to false and wrote a null billing address. Confirmed on
   the original 6653 row (`billing_address` NULL, `billing_address_same` false).

3. DELETING THE JOB FAILED AND LEFT IT GUTTED. Found while clearing 6653 to re-test.
   `CallLogDetail.handleDelete()` preflighted active proposals only, then mutated in sequence:
   delete work types → detach soft-deleted proposals → delete `call_log`. Soft-deleted INVOICES
   also hold `call_log_id`, and unlike proposals that column is NOT NULL, so they cannot be
   detached. The delete died on `invoices_call_log_id_fkey` — after the first two steps had
   already committed. Job still present, work types gone (0 rows), proposal `4469e0ff`
   orphaned, and a raw Postgres constraint string in a browser alert as the only explanation.

DECISIONS
---------
D1. THE IMPORT WIZARD DOES NOT OVERWRITE EXISTING CUSTOMER CONTACT DATA WITH BLANKS. An archive
    import is a low-trust source — its job is to bring a historical job forward, not to be
    authoritative about a customer who already exists in the system. Blank contact phone/email
    are skipped rather than written as null. NewInquiryWizard behaves differently (writes
    unconditionally) and that is correct THERE, because a rep filling the New Inquiry wizard is
    stating current fact. Do not "harmonize" these two without deciding which trust level
    applies; they differ on purpose.

D2. THE PICKER'S billingSame IS NOT THIS WIZARD'S billingSame. Rather than rename either field,
    the fix drops the key at the boundary, with a comment at both the destructure and the JSX.
    Renaming `ImportToLiveWizard.form.billingSame` → `billingAddressSame` is the real cleanup
    and is the better long-term move, but it touches the call_log insert and the addresses step
    and was out of scope for a live-blocking fix. If anyone touches this step again, do the
    rename — the collision is the kind that returns.

D3. THE JOB-DELETE FIX IS CLIENT-SIDE ONLY, AND KNOWINGLY NOT AIRTIGHT. The correct fix is one
    atomic RPC (`delete_call_log(id)`) so a blocker can never leave a half-emptied job. That is
    a `command-suite-db` migration, which escalates a /fix into plan-mode ceremony while Chris
    was blocked on a smoke test. Preflight-everything-first closes the actual damage window —
    nothing mutates until every known blocker has cleared — but a concurrent write between the
    preflight and the delete can still interleave. Flagged to Chris at the time; he took the
    client-side fix. See NEXT SESSION POINTERS #2.

D4. DELETED INVOICES GO WITH THE JOB RATHER THAN BLOCKING IT. `invoices.call_log_id` is NOT
    NULL, so the proposal trick (detach and leave the row) is unavailable. The options were:
    block the delete forever, or take the invoice with it. Chose the latter, because a
    soft-deleted invoice on a job that is itself being deleted has no remaining reader — but
    the confirm dialog names the invoice numbers so it is never a surprise.
