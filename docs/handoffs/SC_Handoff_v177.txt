SESSION SUMMARY
---------------
Chris, looking at invoice 10146: "On my header, the invoice job number and due date are on the
right and move all over the place depending on what date is put into it. They should not be
moving left to right. They should only move up and down. I swear we just fixed this."

He was right that we'd just fixed it — on the PROPOSAL header, 2026-07-27, commit 15fdcc2. The
same bug, in the same shape, was still sitting in the invoice header. That is the headline of
this session: a per-surface fix that never got swept across surfaces, found ten days later by
the same person hitting the same annoyance on a different screen.

Fixing it exposed three more, each found by looking at the screen rather than by searching:

1. THE BLOCK DRIFTED BECAUSE ITS WIDTH WAS ITS CONTENT'S WIDTH. `space-between` pins the right
   block's RIGHT edge to the card edge. The block had auto width, so the longest string in it —
   in practice the due date — set the width, and the width set where the left edge landed. A
   longer date genuinely moved the whole block sideways.

2. THE ENTIRE COMPANY HEADER WAS BLANK, AND HAD BEEN IN BOTH SCREENSHOTS. No company name, no
   tagline, no phone, no website, no license, and the logo quietly falling back to the bundled
   /hdsp-logo.png. Chris read this as a preview-deploy artifact. It was not. See DECISIONS D2 —
   this is the most interesting bug of the session and the one most likely to come back.

3. THE MAILING ADDRESS WASN'T THERE AT ALL. Chris, mid-session: "We also need to add the High
   Desert Surface Preps address in here, probably in the top right, so that people know where to
   mail a check. I hate mixing two fixes in one terminal session, but this is where we're at."
   Internally that's a tenant_config read. For the CUSTOMER-facing page it wasn't available at
   any price — get_public_tenant_config() didn't return the address columns. The one audience
   that actually needs a remit-to address was the one audience that couldn't be shown it.

4. JOB # WAS PRINTING THE JOB NAME. Surfaced when Chris opened invoice 10127 after the centering
   fix landed: "6897 - Plenium Builders Virginia Palmer Elementary - Polish" wrapped to two
   ragged centered lines under a label that says JOB #. The block hadn't moved — it had WRAPPED.
   invoices.job_id stores the whole display string. 10146's job name is "STY 4", short enough
   that the problem was invisible on the invoice we'd been staring at all session.

DECISIONS
---------
D1. CENTER, NOT RIGHT-ALIGNED — AND CHRIS OVERRODE ME ON IT. The first fix mirrored the proposal
    header: fixed 200px width, still right-anchored. Chris said center. I pushed back once —
    right-aligned would share a hard edge with the teal rule, the LINE TOTAL column and the
    AMOUNT DUE box, where centered ties to nothing — and offered both. He restated it plainly:
    "All I want you to do is take the invoice, the job number, and the due date. I want those to
    move to the center of the page. Do you understand that?" Centered it is. Do not re-litigate
    this in a polish pass; it is a stated preference, not an oversight.

D2. THE BLANK HEADER WAS A POISONED CACHE, AND THE FIX IS NOT "RETRY". Worth reading in full,
    because the shape recurs. tenant_config's SELECT policy is `id = get_user_tenant_id()` for
    the `authenticated` role only — anon has no policy at all. Login.jsx:15 calls
    getTenantConfig() on mount to show the company name on the login screen, i.e. while still
    anonymous. That read correctly returns nothing. config.js then did
    `_cache = { ...DEFAULTS, ...data }` with data === null, storing all-empty DEFAULTS in a
    module-level variable that lives as long as the SPA does. Every consumer that asked
    afterward — invoice preview, proposal PDF modal, WTC calculator, InvoicePaidPage — got the
    blanks. It looked intermittent because landing directly on an authenticated route (a
    refresh on /invoices) never mounts Login, so the first read comes from an authenticated
    context and succeeds. THE LESSON: a cache that stores failures is a cache that converts a
    transient permission state into a permanent one. Anywhere a module-level cache fronts an
    RLS-gated read, the miss must not be cached and the cache must clear on auth change.
    Both are now done here; nothing else in the codebase caches an RLS read this way (checked).

D3. THE MIGRATION WAS PUSHED TO PROD MID-SESSION, WITH EXPLICIT PERMISSION. Rehearsed first per
    the standing MIG-1 discipline: check-migration-safety.sh (A + B PASS), then
    ./scripts/rehearse.sh against a prod-shaped throwaway — all nine fingerprints matched prod,
    candidate applied clean, and the post-candidate anon-exposure check confirmed no new anon
    exposure. Only then asked Chris, who said push. Verified live afterward by calling the
    function AS the anon role. The address is business mailing information already printed on
    every proposal and invoice that leaves the building — the same thing a customer reads off
    the paper copy — so widening the anon-visible subset is not a disclosure.

D4. JOB # SHOWS THE NUMBER AND DROPS THE JOB NAME. A field labeled JOB # printing
    "6897 - Plenium Builders Virginia Palmer Elementary - Polish" is wrong independent of the
    wrapping. Took the leading segment, matching what ProposalPDFModal already does with
    display_job_number. The cost, flagged to Chris and accepted ("yes i like that"): 10146 now
    reads "7215", not "7215 - STY 4". The jobsite address and work description already identify
    the job. If the name is ever wanted back, it belongs on its own line under the number — not
    crammed into the number field.

D5. SCREEN AND PDF CAN STILL DISAGREE ON JOB #, KNOWINGLY. invoicePdf.js prefers
    `callLog.subcontractor_job_no` (the GC's own job number, e.g. DA Builders' 6359) over ours;
    the two HTML surfaces have no access to that column. Matching them means adding
    job_number / subcontractor_job_no to the public page's call_log select, which means granting
    those columns to anon — a migration AND a change to the rehearsal anon-exposure allowlist,
    for a cosmetic field. Not taken. Only shows up on jobs that HAVE a subcontractor job number;
    none of today's did.

D6. THE LEFT COLUMN'S PDF WRAP IS A CONSEQUENCE OF THE CENTERING, NOT AN OPPORTUNISTIC FIX.
    Centering moved the right block's left edge from x=326 to x=206. The left column was
    unwrapped doc.text() and had ~120pt more room before. drawWrapped() was required to keep the
    centering from creating an overlap bug in the emailed PDF. It is not scope creep; it is the
    other half of the same change.
