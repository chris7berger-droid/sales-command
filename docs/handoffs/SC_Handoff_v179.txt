SESSION SUMMARY
───────────────
Sales Command could only invoice a proposal as a PERCENTAGE of a fixed price.
T&M has no fixed price — only a rate — so there was nothing to take a percentage
of. Proposal 7215 P7 encodes $105/$125/$150 per hour as work types with
regular_hours = 1, which makes a $105/hr rate look like a $105 line item. That
line could produce exactly $105 of invoicing, for the life of the job.

HDSP already solves this on paper: the T&M Authorization is filled out in the
field, priced, signed by the GC's superintendent, and turned in. This build makes
that paper billable — one invoice line per work day, carrying date, crew size,
area, and hours split by rate class.

Planning ran two adversarial rounds and one model replacement:
  round 1  T&M ticket as its own object (3 tables, 12 policies, own routes)
           → 23 findings, 1 Critical. Model replaced.
  round 2  the invoice line IS the ticket
           → 14 findings, 0 Critical. −39%. Model validated.
  v3       billing half only; rate-card exclusion split to F44.
The design question was settled by DELETING the ticket object, not by defending
it. What remained was concentrated in one mechanism, which became its own job.

DECISIONS
─────────
1. THE INVOICE LINE IS THE TICKET. No ticket object. Round 1 built one; it
   generated 3 tables, 12 policies, its own routes, and the plurality of the
   findings. Deleting it dropped the count 39% and dissolved the Critical.

2. RATE CARDS STILL COUNT TOWARD PROPOSAL TOTALS. Deliberate scope boundary.
   P7 still reads $28,379.64 including $380 of phantom hours — identical to
   production today. That is F44, now T1. See NEXT SESSION POINTERS.

3. NOT-TO-EXCEED IS PER INVOICE (per week), not per job. A GC may impose a cap
   partway through; billing is weekly, so the week is where the call is made.
   A job-level ceiling was considered and rejected — no live use for it.

4. RETENTION IS ALL-OR-NOTHING on an invoice, hours included. Chris: some
   customers do hold retention on T&M. Retaining on contract work only is not
   expressible and was NOT filed — no customer needs it.

5. VOID-AND-REPLACE DROPS T&M LINES ENTIRELY — rows and dollars.
   This REVERSES what the round-2 audit asked for (it wanted the day fields
   carried forward). Reason: a day row is a TRANSCRIPTION of a signed paper. If
   an invoice is being voided, the correctness of that transcription is exactly
   what may be in question — reissuing should mean re-reading the paper, not
   copying a previous reading. Compounding it: a T&M line's hours are
   preserve-only once billed, so a carried-over wrong figure could not be
   corrected. Percent/SOV/archive lines still copy: derived, cannot be
   independently wrong.

6. JOB TOTALS IS ONE LEDGER, NOT TWO BOXES. First build put a T&M row beside the
   contract figures and read "BILLED $0" directly above "BILLED $6,765".
   Design research: every construction job summary surveyed uses one running
   ledger (original + approved changes = revised value, then billed against it) —
   G702, Mastt, Foundation, Procore. Billed T&M hours grow the job's value
   exactly as an approved change order does. Two boxes would still need two
   "Billed" labels — the collision moves rather than resolves.

7. FIXTURE NUMBERED BELOW THE LIVE SEQUENCE. Learned the hard way — see PROD
   DATA WRITES.

8. THE PDF BRANCH WENT IN THOUGH NOTHING REACHES IT. invoicePdf.js has no
   regular-invoice caller today. The branch was added anyway rather than filed as
   a warning, because wiring the future caller would otherwise inherit a renderer
   that prints $105 as the value of a $4,460 line.
