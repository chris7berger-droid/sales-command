███ THE VISION IN ONE BREATH █████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████

Chris flagged his statement as one of the most important he's made for this app (captured verbatim in
the doc). Core: the Home screen must DRIVE estimators to the work the BUSINESS needs, not their default
habits — and it must feel "like a video game, obvious and easy," engaging not stressing/boring/isolating.

ONE screen for everyone (business-need driven, not roles). Breathes top→bottom:
  1. YOU (hero) — a win that reads their month; effort on a bad month, results on a good one. Always #1.
  2. YOUR MONEY — month progress bar + moving pace marker + clickable donut (work type / big-vs-small /
     booked-vs-remaining). Gaps spoken as opportunity, never deficit.
  3. THE BUSINESS IN THE OPEN — crew runway (everyone sees, manager enters) + shared $100K goal
     thermometer (no ranking). Runway slides up + turns tension-color when critical; hero stays put.
  4. YOUR BOOK — tappable, dollars-forward pipeline scoreboard; drill straight into each stage.
  5. WHAT YOU OWE — follow-ups = self-set dates + bids due (NO auto call-backs); celebrates when clear.
  6. WHERE TO HUNT — opportunity finder (serves one + Refresh through 10 pre-built angles, shows its
     work) + existing dormant/gone-quiet call lists, spiced with a dollar number.
