SPUN OUT — the Add Job / duplicating-jobs issue (was F58)
================================================================================
Root cause of the "Dave Lee #10252 shows crew on the grid but 'Crew not assigned'
on the Next Up card": there are TWO jobs rows for job# 10252 — job_id 1191 (real,
call_log 3847, fully crewed) and job_id 1278 (phantom manual add, call_log_id
NULL, 1 stray "Bash Dave" assignment). The grid counts crew by the job row; the
Next Up card checks crew through the call_log link, which the phantom lacks → the
two disagree. This is NOT a display bug — it's bad data from manual Add Job blind-
INSERTing.

It's SYSTEMIC, not a one-off (verified against prod tonight):
  • 42 job#s have duplicate jobs rows
  • 67 of 289 active jobs have NULL call_log_id
  • of the 42 dup job#s: 8 have a phantom (null-call_log manual add); 34 have
    MULTIPLE Sales-linked rows (likely change orders legitimately sharing a job#
    — must be distinguished from true dupes).
