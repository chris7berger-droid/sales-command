DECISIONS / CHOICES MADE

- Relabel, don't rebuild, for 4 of 5 KPIs (Chris ratified). "Billed YTD"→"Sold YTD", "Scheduled to Bill (This Month)"→"Next 30 Days", "Average Margin"→"Avg Quoted Margin (Sold YTD)", "Hot Opportunities"→"Bids Out". Each read plausibly but meant something other than its label; rebuilding two of them (true billed, realized margin) needs data that doesn't flow yet (AR-live invoices; field actuals). Honest labels now, rebuild when the data exists.
- Outstanding AR was the one real math bug, not a label choice. It summed gross invoice `amount` (incl. retention held + discounts); fixed to net collectable via the canonical netOfInvoice + isActiveInvoice/isSent/isPaid predicates, excluding retention-release rows. One Sold-YTD population now drives Sold YTD, Jobs YTD, and Avg Quoted Margin so they can't drift.
- AR KPI drill-downs are display-only (audit B2). Home's AR numbers come from the live `invoices` table, but the AR module still gates on its QuickBooks-import store, so a click would land on "upload a QB export." Disabled the click until AR reads `invoices` (§4 deferred).
- Fixed the double-booked count at the SHARED calc, not a Home-only twin. It also corrects Schedule Command's own "At a Glance" conflict number (HomePanels.jsx reads the same value), which was inflated the same way. Kept conflict-scope (_BOARD_STATUSES, incl. On Hold) separate from crew-shortfall (_SCHEDULING_STATUSES, excl. On Hold) — conflicts follow what the board draws crew for; shortfall does not.
