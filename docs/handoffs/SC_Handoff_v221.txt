SESSION SUMMARY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Fixed B103 (T1): the schedule was hiding real, crewed jobs. B86 had hidden every
job with no Sales link (null call_log_id) on the premise "null link = phantom" —
wrong for jobs that have real crew assigned. Symptom: Weekly Crew Capacity counted
crew the board/calendar/exports didn't show; crew tooltips showed a raw id
("Job 1054"); Daily Crew Status printed "—" for crew who WERE assigned.

Started with a narrower fix (hide only if unlinked AND no crew) but Chris changed
the model mid-session: the app must NEVER be the one deciding what he doesn't see,
because real people are never assigned to a job that shouldn't be looked at, and
hidden bugs are the dangerous ones. Final model: SHOW EVERY active job; FLAG the
unlinked ones instead of hiding. Merged to main and deployed; Chris confirmed the
live board looks good.

DECISIONS (this session)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  • Model: SHOW EVERYTHING, FLAG THE BROKEN — never hide. Supersedes the initial
    "hide unless crewed" fix. Rationale (Chris): the app shouldn't decide what he
    can't see; real crew proves a job is real; hidden bugs are the dangerous ones
    (his standing invisible=dangerous discipline). An empty phantom now shows too,
    flagged with ⚠ and no crew — a nudge to clean it up, not something buried.
  • Flag style (Chris picked): keep the job's real name/number and add a ⚠; only
    fall back to the "Needs fixing" text when the job has no name at all.
  • Flag centralized in normalizeJob so all schedule surfaces show it identically
    from one place (no per-view edits, no drift).
  • No T6 security review: read-path display change only — no access rules, grants,
    auth, money, or public/anon page touched.
  • Pushed straight to prod on Chris's "push" without a separate browser smoke
    first (build clean + prod-data verified); Chris confirmed the live board after.
