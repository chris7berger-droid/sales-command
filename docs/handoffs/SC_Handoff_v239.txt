--------------------------------
- Preserve B110's distinct trip rows, editors, UUID assignment attribution,
  sibling-safe removals and conservative unidentified legacy row.

  error/retry handling, and clickable Jobs Starting/Ending/Needing Crew bubbles.
- Reconcile summary model with crewScheduleRows / crewRowStaffing / crewRowNames.
  An overlap alone no longer creates an unknown staffing target. Each trip has
  its own requirement, inheritance and assigned names, just like its board row.
  Count each Sales job once per bubble, retaining all qualifying trip details.
  Unidentified history never creates a planned trip or fills both siblings.
- Popup links locate and expand the exact row key, not a job_id shared by several
  trip rows. The right editor opens, including parent defaults where applicable.
- Draft guards track editor/row keys, not job_id. Cancelling one trip's draft
  cannot clear another trip's guard. Guarded week changes and summary links
  preserve unsaved work.
- Unknown-target details explicitly show recorded assigned counts. Past-week
  headers do not display a requirement-cleanup warning. Historical details remain
  accessible from the Needing Crew popup, with neutral copy.
- Daily header figures count unique active people per day; the same person on
  two trips does not inflate capacity usage. Trip-specific shortages remain
  independent and update alongside the board after staffing changes.
