CHANGES SHIPPED
/crew now has two modes:
- Weekly send — existing Monday–Sunday long text, week picker, start
  notes, Share week / Copy week. Unchanged.
- Midweek Update — read-only compact remaining-week text from today
  through Friday of the calendar week containing today. Week navigation
  cannot send a past/future midweek range.

Compact message:
UPDATED CREW SCHEDULE — ABBREVIATED
[employee display name]
Current schedule from today forward. Schedule may change as jobs shift.
Then remaining-day lines:
TUE 9/15 — JOB #1842 — with Mike Jones
THU 9/17 — (OFF — MAY CHANGE)

Job # and coworkers come from live assignments + trip links (same
authority as weekly send). Full crewDisplayName names. No coworkers
omits the with … portion. Multiple assignments on one day get separate
lines. Assignment wins over scheduled-off on the same day.

(OFF — MAY CHANGE) only from stored crew_status.status === "scheduled-off".
Do not infer Off from a blank assignment. Legacy off / Call In, Sick,
No Show, and blank days are omitted.

Desktop Schedules.jsx was not modified. No assignment writes. No
crew_status writes. No remap of legacy off.
